Problem Identification

Disaster Challenges & Government Gaps[span_12](start_span)[span_12](end_span)

Delayed Early Warnings[span_13](start_span)[span_13](end_span)

Lack of real-time, localized disaster predictions for cloudbursts, flash floods, and landslides, leading to zero preparation time for citizens[span_14](start_span)[span_14](end_span).

Network Blackouts[span_15](start_span)[span_15](end_span)

Standard cellular and internet towers collapse during natural calamities, creating total communication blackout in affected zones[span_16](start_span)[span_16](end_span).

Mismanaged Relief Supply[span_17](start_span)[span_17](end_span)

Inefficient manual distribution leads to bottlenecks, hoardings, and delayed medical aid reaching victims in high-priority disaster pockets[span_18](start_span)[span_18](end_span).

Current Government System Gaps[span_19](start_span)[span_19](end_span)

Over-Reliance on Reactive Measures: Focus is on post-disaster compensation and rescue rather than proactive AI-driven prevention[span_20](start_span)[span_20](end_span).

Failure in Last-Mile Execution: High-level policy decisions and warnings fail to filter down swiftly to village Panchayats and rural first-responders[span_21](start_span)[span_21](end_span).

Unplanned Infrastructure: Lax enforcement on riverbed encroachments and mountain road construction increases flood/landslide severity[span_22](start_span)[span_22](end_span).

Lack of Citizen Drill Protocols: Minimal community-level mock drills leave citizens unaware of immediate survival steps during calamities[span_23](start_span)[span_23](end_span).

System Specifications

Technical System Architecture[span_39](start_span)[span_39](end_span)

AI & Data Intelligence Layer[span_40](start_span)[span_40](end_span)

Utilizes Convolutional Neural Networks (CNN) for aerial debris scanning and LSTM models for flood level predictions[span_41](start_span)[span_41](end_span).

Hardware & IoT Grid[span_42](start_span)[span_42](end_span)

Deployed on low-power LoRaWAN nodes and Raspberry Pi edge devices connected to water pressure and vibration sensors[span_43](start_span)[span_43](end_span).

  • ✓ Real-time Telemetry: Sub-second sensor data aggregation[span_44](start_span)[span_44](end_span)
  • ✓ Edge Inference: AI models execute directly on-drone without cloud[span_45](start_span)[span_45](end_span)

Offline Mesh Communication (Zero Grid Reliance)[span_46](start_span)[span_46](end_span)

Creates an ad-hoc peer-to-peer LoRa mesh network spanning up to 15 kilometers[span_47](start_span)[span_47](end_span). Smartphone distress signals hop through nodes to reach the central command center without active internet[span_48](start_span)[span_48](end_span).
