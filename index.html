Problem Identification

Disaster Challenges & Government Gaps

Delayed Early Warnings

Lack of localized, real-time predictions for flash floods, cloudbursts and landslides prevents timely evacuations. Current forecasting is coarse-grained and slow to reach local stakeholders.

Network Blackouts

Cell towers and fiber are often downed during severe events, creating communication blackholes that block distress signalling, situational updates and coordination between agencies.

Mismanaged Relief Supply

Manual distribution and poor tracking lead to hoarding, duplication, and missed high-priority cases. Lack of a verified last-mile process undermines fairness and speed.

Current Government System Gaps

Over-Reliance on Reactive Measures:
Policy focus and funding often target post-disaster rescue and compensation rather than continuous monitoring, localized early-warning systems and AI-driven proactive mitigation.
Last-Mile Execution Failure:
National and state directives frequently fail to reach Panchayats and remote field teams in time; there is no standardized drill or distribution protocol in many rural areas.
Unplanned Infrastructure:
Unregulated construction in floodplains and steep slopes increases disaster severity; enforcement and monitoring are inconsistent.
Lack of Citizen Drill Protocols:
Few communities conduct regular, realistic drills; citizens are thus often unaware of immediate survival and assembly steps during calamities.

System Specifications

Technical System Architecture

AI & Data Intelligence Layer

Utilizes Convolutional Neural Networks (CNN) for aerial debris/thermal target detection and LSTM models for time-series flood-level forecasting. Ensemble models combine telemetry and meteorological forecasts for localized alerts.

  • ✓ CNN for thermal/visual person detection on drones
  • ✓ LSTM/GRU for short-horizon flood prediction (sub-hour)
  • ✓ Model compression & edge inference (TinyML / ONNX runtimes)

Hardware & IoT Grid

Low-power LoRaWAN nodes and Raspberry Pi / Jetson Nano edge devices aggregate water pressure, vibration and rainfall telemetry with local preprocessing and event-driven uploads.

  • ✓ Sub-second telemetry aggregation where connectivity permits
  • ✓ Edge inference on drones and gateways to reduce latency

Offline Mesh Communication

Ad-hoc P2P LoRa mesh with opportunistic smartphone relays and portable gateways ensures message relay up to ~15 km in cooperative deployments. Prioritised distress messages use compact encodings for efficiency.
