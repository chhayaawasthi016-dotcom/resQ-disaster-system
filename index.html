1. Sequence: Challenges & Governance Gaps

Understanding the core bottlenecks in current emergency response systems

Major Disaster Bottlenecks

Delayed Early Warnings

Lack of real-time, localized predictions for cloudbursts, flash floods, and landslides, leading to zero preparation time for citizens.

Network Blackouts

Standard cellular and internet towers collapse during natural calamities, creating total communication blackouts in affected zones.

Mismanaged Relief Supply

Inefficient manual distribution leads to bottlenecks, hoarding, and delayed medical aid reaching high-priority disaster pockets.

Current Government Operational Gaps

Reactive over Proactive

Most administrative machinery focuses on post-disaster compensation and rescue rather than AI-driven prevention and early evacuation.

Last-Mile Execution Failure

High-level policy decisions and warnings fail to filter down swiftly to village-level Panchayats and rural first-responders.

Unplanned Expansion & Protocols

Lax enforcement on riverbed encroachments and lack of citizen mock drills leave communities unaware during sudden natural calamities.
