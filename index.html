Major Disaster Challenges

Key operational bottlenecks identified in current emergency management workflows

Delayed Early Warnings

Lack of real-time, localized disaster predictions for cloudbursts, flash floods, and landslides, leading to zero preparation time for citizens.

Network Blackouts

Standard cellular and internet towers collapse during natural calamities, creating total communication blackouts in affected zones.

Mismanaged Relief Supply

Inefficient manual distribution leads to bottlenecks, hoarding, and delayed medical aid reaching high-priority disaster pockets.

Current Government Gaps

Over-Reliance on Reactive Measures: Focus on post-disaster compensation rather than proactive AI-driven prevention and evacuation.

Failure in Last-Mile Execution: Policy decisions and warnings fail to filter down swiftly to village Panchayats and rural first-responders.

Unplanned Infrastructure Expansion: Lax enforcement on riverbed encroachments exponentially increases flood and landslide severity.

Technical System Architecture

Deep learning models and IoT hardware integration

Hardware & IoT Grid

Deployed on low-power LoRaWAN nodes and Raspberry Pi edge devices connected to water pressure and vibration sensors.

  • Real-time Telemetry: Sub-second sensor data aggregation.
  • Edge Inference: AI models execute directly on-drone without cloud dependency.

Offline Mesh Communication

When towers collapse, an ad-hoc peer-to-peer LoRa mesh network spans up to 15km, passing smartphone SOS signals automatically to command centers.
